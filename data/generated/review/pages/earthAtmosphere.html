Three scenarios (S1, S2, and S6) include solar radiation

One scenario (S1) includes a continuously broadcast optical
signal toward nearby habitable planets, specifically for the
purpose of establishing contact with other life (i.e, a laser

These optical transmissions are assumed to occur at 1.064 μm

S5, S6, S8, S9, and S10), and one scenario includes the use of
laser-propelled nanocraft; however, much work needs to be
done to estimate if HWO would be able to see these features in
the observed spectra. As this requires some extensive and
careful analysis, we leave it for future work.
