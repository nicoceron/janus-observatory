# Superseded observer optical-axis review

This intermediate pass was accepted against its original optical-axis brief, then superseded by
`../janus-observer-spectral-v3/` after product review found that entering the telescope produced an
implausible camera move and repeated the planet instead of revealing instrument data. The sheet is
retained as rejection evidence; it is not the current runtime behavior.

- The 260svh observer article remains the scroll runway; no click or autonomous timeline starts
  the shot.
- The v4 character performance produces a readable anticipation, full-body lean, two-arm action,
  blink, and antenna secondary motion before the camera move begins.
- The 3D room arches, floor, wall, and evidence beam are absent. The observer is isolated against
  the persistent procedural star field and a restrained deep-space gradient.
- The camera arcs around the telescope without clipping through the alien, faces the large
  objective head-on, and terminates on the exported objective-to-eyepiece optical axis.
- The centered optical mask hides the final near-plane crossing, then the following story step
  resolves into the structured ocular view.
- The animation and camera remain reversible under backscroll; reduced motion retains the static
  DOM/WebGL fallback behavior.

The Blender asset sheet and browser scroll sheet both contain no automatic difference warnings.
