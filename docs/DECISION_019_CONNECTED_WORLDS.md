# Decision 019: connected roads and visible Solar System locations

2026-09-08, user-directed correction of Decisions 017–018 on `codex/janus-first-light`.

The user clarified that “rows” meant roads and paths on the planets. Inspection found two failures.
Decorative road strips remained independently of the new activity routes. The activity routes
themselves used approximate building approaches, while entrances faced arbitrary directions and
road widths did not account for vehicle widths. Some background structures crossed the routes.

Remove the decorative road builder. Share landmark positions, orient the relevant entrances toward
their routes, and generate roads between explicit local access points. Offline planning checks the
whole carriageway against terrain, cliffs and landmark footprints. Its width derives from the
actual actor geometry. Forecourts continue to the modeled entrance or loading face. Background
structures and props use the same prepared route corridor for clearance. The S6 maintenance walker
travels on its existing pressure shell without a painted road. S8 has enough separation between its
recovery clearing and salvage yard to accommodate the machine. Roads, vehicles and exact settlement
layouts remain interpretive art; these are not source-reported transport networks.

Luna, Mars, Venus and extended-system models were not deleted. Decision 017 had moved them into the
explorer, and mobile styling hid the companion shortcut buttons. That made their continued presence
too difficult to discover. Show every source-selected location directly in each scenario's story
controls, on desktop and mobile. Selecting a location gives it the full-size spatial portrait in
the existing canvas; “Explore in detail” opens that same location. Closing preserves the portrait
and restores keyboard focus. Non-Earth explorer views omit unrelated Earth object buttons.

Keep the existing `systemPortrait` source rule: a body is selected from a positive published Table 6
activity field, with surface and orbital activity kept distinct; extended locations come from
Table 8. Empty cells do not establish absence. S4 and S7 therefore have no added destination selector,
and S8 retains Luna. The canonical release, scenario summaries and SourceRefs are unchanged. The
readable “Selected off-world footprint” and source links remain in the static story. Each selectable
portrait also has a structured DOM description. Models and dimensions are interpretive artwork.

No dependency, canonical schema, scientific-value or architecture change. The scene keeps one
canvas, native scrolling and the existing reduced-motion/fallback modes. Mobile chapter offsets
account for the destination controls so they sit between the globe and narrative. Hidden models do
not advance animation, and changed portrait geometry is disposed when replaced. Original-art route
JSON is regenerated by its script, never manually edited.

Documentation consulted before implementation: `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md`, Decisions 016–018, the
installed Next.js `use client` reference, and the existing canonical Table 6/Table 8 portrait
selector and its provenance. Verification, affected files, rights and limitations are recorded in
[the connected-worlds review](qa/connected-worlds/REPORT.md).
