# Decision 030 — Continuous flight and readable crops

The user wants flying objects to circle each miniature world and rejected the repeated brown crop bars because they resemble wooden pallets.

All four airborne actors (opening Earth and S3 aircraft, S1 patrol drone, S5 imagined pollinator) use a continuous inclined great circle at constant angular speed. Their local forward axis follows the tangent and their underside faces the world. The duplicated final route sample has the same position and orientation as the first, so wrapping time never causes a reversal or teleport. The planet naturally occludes the far half of each circuit. Authored roof envelopes establish clearance; clouds retain their slower weather drift. These are interpretive miniature flights, not physical satellite orbits or claims from canonical science.

Airfield scenery retains supported ground sites independently of flight motion. Its actual mesh reserves ground clearance, rather than airborne route endpoints. Boats and surface vehicles keep their existing destination-based activity. Opening Earth fields use open dark soil beds, individual leafy crops and green or golden stalks; no rails or corner posts. Mobile includes planted plots too.

The existing Blender pipeline receives the same route samples and timing as Three.js. Updated editable sources and losslessly transported GLBs retain semantic parts. No dependency, canonical schema, resolution policy or runtime physics change. Offline generation and serial two-thread Blender builds retain the existing resource guard.
