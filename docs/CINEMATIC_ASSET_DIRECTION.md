# Cinematic asset direction and evidence boundary

2026-09-04 · implementation of Decision 011.

The authored scenario profiles are material interpretations, not remote-sensing simulations.
All ten preserve registered Solar System Scope Earth geography and one solar direction. Land-only material
variation is deliberately restrained; the source does not provide future settlement maps.
Artificial illumination is gated by each scenario's canonical Earth field. Missing values
are not converted to measured zero. Ocular views suppress illustrative emission enhancement.

S9 additionally receives an original Blender-authored stellar cutaway, gated by the
canonical Table 7 `dyson_sphere` field. The stellar structure is separate from the retained
Earth, absent from ocular views, and labeled hypothetical geometry/not to scale. Its panel
count, layout, star appearance, and relative size do not encode scientific values.

Each profile is grounded in the corresponding `canonicalSummary` from generated morphology,
JANUS-PAPER-01, arXiv:2409.00067v3, page 11, section 3.2.1, scenario row S1–S10.
The visible narrative is rendered from that sourced field. Population, energy, governance,
technology, and all five Figure 6 results retain their own canonical values and source links.

## Original observer

`assets/sources/janus-cinematic/build_observatory.py` authors a fictional ten-bone
character, continuous garment, constrained arm contacts, a short focus/settle action,
open optical tube, architecture, normal bitmaps, and two camera tracks. It contains no
third-party character or telescope mesh. The anatomy is a design choice, not a claim
about extraterrestrial life. The instrument is not a literal HWO/radio/LIFE/SGL/probe design.

The editable Blender source, uncompressed GLB, and generated shot metadata are retained.
The public derivative uses Meshopt with 16-bit positions and 12-bit normal/weight precision;
no mesh simplification is applied. Rendered posters use the authored camera views. Their
reference Earth uses the separately admitted Solar System Scope base image (CC BY 4.0) and requires its existing credit.

## Review workspace

`media/cinematic-review` is an isolated Remotion project, not a production dependency.
It assembles actual browser captures and the authored Blender frames for review. Remotion
4.0.520's [license](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing) permits individuals and
organizations of up to three people to use the free license; a larger organization must
confirm its appropriate company license before adopting this tooling. No paid license,
Firefly generation, upload, or video-credit purchase is part of this implementation.
